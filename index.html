Toponym Extractor

Turns MapReader text detections in pixel coordinates into a latitude/longitude table, using the ground control points in an Allmaps Georeference Annotation. Everything runs in this browser tab — your files are never uploaded anywhere.

Substring match anywhere in the transcribed text, ignoring case and accents — segou finds both SEGOU and Ségouba, and bou finds Diambour.

Allmaps ground control points use image pixels with Y growing downward from the top-left corner. MapReader output that has passed through a GIS often has negative or upward Y instead, which has to be corrected or the result comes out mirrored.
